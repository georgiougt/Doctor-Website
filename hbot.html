What is HBOT?

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) involves breathing pure oxygen in a pressurized environment. Under these conditions, your lungs can gather much more oxygen than would be possible breathing pure oxygen at normal air pressure. This extra oxygen helps fight bacteria and stimulates the release of substances called growth factors and stem cells, which promote healing.
