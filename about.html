About Dr. Savvas Savvoulas

Paediatrician & Family Physician

Dr. Savvas Savvoulas is a highly respected medical professional with over 15 years of experience in Pediatric and Family Medicine. Based in Cyprus, he has dedicated his career to providing compassionate, evidence-based care to patients of all ages.

After completing his medical degree and specialization, Dr. Savvoulas established his private practice with the vision of offering a holistic approach to health. He believes that effective treatment goes beyond just addressing symptoms—it requires understanding the patient's lifestyle, environment, and personal history.

Philosophy of Care

"My goal is to be a partner in my patients' health journey. Whether it's a newborn's first check-up or managing chronic conditions for a grandparent, I strive to create a comfortable environment where open communication and trust form the foundation of our medical relationship."

Specialization

  • Pediatric Intensive Care
  • Preventive Medicine
  • Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (Certified Specialist)
