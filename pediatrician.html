Our Pediatric Services

As a dedicated pediatrician, Dr. Savvas provides comprehensive care tailored to the unique needs of children at every stage of growth.

👶 Newborn & Infant Care

Thorough examinations for newborns, breastfeeding support, and growth monitoring to ensure your baby gets the best start in life.

💉 Vaccinations & Immunizations

Following the national immunization schedule to protect your child from preventable diseases with a gentle approach.

🤒 Sick Visits & Acute Care

Quick and effective treatment for common childhood illnesses like fevers, infections, allergies, and minor injuries.

📏 Developmental Screenings

Regular assessments to track physical, mental, and social development, ensuring any concerns are addressed early.
